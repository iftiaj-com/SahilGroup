Who We Are

Driving Innovation in the RMG Sector Since 2003

Started with a vision by Mr. Kazi Nazrul Islam to revolutionize the clothing industry, Sahil Group Bangladesh has grown from a modest sewing setup into one of the top garment manufacturing operations, striving to be ranked among the top 10 garments in Bangladesh.

Today, we offer a completely vertical structure incorporating knitting, dyeing, and fabric finishing with state-of-the-art technology from Germany, Taiwan, and the USA. As a premier local B2B supplier for Knitting & Dyeing in Bangladesh, we are a trusted supply chain partner for leading global brands in Europe and America.
