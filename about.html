Company Profile

Everything started with a clothing organization in 2003 and a man's vision, Mr. Kazi Nazrul Islam, to take a leap in the clothing industry and make Sahil Group one of the main wholesale clothing makers in Bangladesh. Under his energetic leadership, the Sahil Group's business operations have progressed step by step. As a result, we became a pioneer in garment manufacturing in Bangladesh and are extremely well-known for wholesale clothing production in both Europe and America.

Sahil Group began operations in the RMG sector in 2003 with only 18 sewing machines. Under the leadership of its Managing Director, Mr. Kazi Nazrul Islam (Guddu), and a team of dedicated and talented consultants, Sahil Group has emerged as a premier knit garment manufacturer and local B2B supplier for Knitting & Dyeing in Bangladesh. Today, Sahil Group offers a completely advanced structure that incorporates knitting, dyeing, a fabric finishing division with all of the most up-to-date technologies, a few sewing units deeply skillful about the creation of Garments for all age ranges of both gender identities.

Since its beginning, the organization has been trading various types of T-Shirts, Polo Shirts, alongside a wide range of woven fabric to two or three nations, for example, the USA, UK, Denmark, Italy, Spain, and so on, and has built up a decent reputation as a solid seller for our clients in general. The coming days will be more highly competitive as a result of globalization. Furthermore, with its strong moto, Sahil Group will undoubtedly dominate the race.

Our Integrated Ecosystem

Knitting Division

Equipped with high-speed Pailung (Taiwan) and Fukuhara (Japan) circular knitting machines. We produce an extensive range of fabrics including Single Jersey, Rib, Interlock, Fleece, and Pique with unparalleled structural consistency. Our automated quality control sensors reduce fabric defects to near-zero rates.

Dyeing Division

State-of-the-art coloration facility utilizing Fong’s and Tong Gen high-temperature machines. Our in-house spectrophotometer lab ensures exact Pantone matching using exclusively Swiss and German dyestuffs. We employ low-liquor ratio dyeing to minimize water consumption.

Finishing Division

The final touch of quality. Utilizing German Bruckner Stenters and Italian Lafer Compacters, we achieve superior dimensional stability (shrinkage < 5%) and premium hand-feel for both open-width and tubular fabrics. Our relax dryers ensure the natural bounce of the fabric remains intact.

Official BKDOA Member

Sahil Group is a proud registered member of the Bangladesh Knit Dyeing Owners Association (BKDOA), serving as a trusted Knitting and Dyeing Partner for Local Bangladeshi RMG Exporters.

As a premier vertical conglomerate in knitting, dyeing, and finishing, our association with BKDOA underscores our dedication to maintaining high-quality standards and advancing environmental and operational compliance within Bangladesh's Readymade Garment (RMG) sector.

Through our active participation with BKDOA, we align with the association's mission to drive sustainable industrial growth, advocate for responsible manufacturing, and keep pace with the latest technological developments in the dyeing and processing sectors.
