Overview

To meet international buyer requirements and serve as a reliable Knitting and Dyeing Partner for Local Bangladeshi RMG Exporters, our knitting unit is equipped with modern and sophisticated Pailung Circular Knitting Machines from Taiwan.

As a premier knit garment manufacturer, we act as a trusted local B2B supplier for Knitting & Dyeing in Bangladesh, ensuring uninterrupted production with a dedicated gas generator power supply.
