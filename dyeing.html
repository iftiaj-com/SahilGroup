Dyeing Division

Advanced Coloration Technology & Sustainable Processing

Overview

As a premier knit garment manufacturer and local B2B supplier for Knitting & Dyeing in Bangladesh, our dyeing division combines precision engineering with chemical safety. We possess an advanced in-house laboratory to ensure exact Pantone matching.

We serve as a reliable Knitting and Dyeing Partner for Local Bangladeshi RMG Exporters, exclusively using Reactive Dyestuff & Azo-Free Chemicals imported from Germany and Switzerland.

Production Capacity 12+ Tons / Day

Key Features

  • Color Technology Spectrophotometer Lab
  • Dyes Used Reactive & Azo-Free
  • Chemical Origin Germany & Switzerland
  • Finishing Bruckner Stenter (Germany)
  • Slitting Bianco (Italy)
  • Sustainability In-house ETP & WTP
